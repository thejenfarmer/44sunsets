21The Loose Thread — putting the Zeigarnik Effect to work

Zeigarnik (1927): unfinished tasks hold ~2× the mental grip of finished ones — the open loop nags. For ADHD founders that tension is usually the enemy (every open loop nags at once). This concept flips it: when you stop a Deep Work session, the app helps you deliberately leave one loop open — a mid-sentence "thread" naming the exact next physical move. Writing it down discharges tonight's intrusive tension (Masicampo & Baumeister: a specific plan quiets the loop), and tomorrow the same tension becomes pull instead of dread: re-entry starts mid-motion, zero decisions. One thread at a time, ever. It never ages, never shows a date, and vanishes the moment it's picked up. Copy pass (Calm school): the effect is never named on-screen — screens describe the feeling ("it keeps tugging"), normalize it, and frame the thread as relief tonight and a way back in tomorrow.
